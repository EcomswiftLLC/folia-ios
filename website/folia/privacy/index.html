Privacy Policy

App: PDF Scanner & Editor: Folia ("Folia")

Company: Ecom Swift LLC, 30 N Gould St #37906, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States

Contact: support@ecomswiftllc.com

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what Folia does with information. The short version: your documents are scanned, read and edited on your device and are never uploaded, and the only outside party that receives any data is the advertising service that pays for the app.

1. The short version

  • Folia has no account and no sign-in.
  • Your scans, PDFs, signature and the text found on your pages stay on your device. We do not upload them and we cannot see them.
  • Folia is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device and ad data to show and measure ads. You are asked for your consent where the law requires it.
  • We do not run our own analytics, and there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
  • Folia does not track you. The privacy manifest of the app declares that it does not use data for tracking.

2. Documents and other information that stay on your device

Camera. When you choose Scan, Folia opens Apple's document scanner, which uses your camera to capture pages. The camera is used only while the scanner is open. Pictures are stored inside the app on your device.

Photos and Files. You choose which photos, PDFs and picture files to add. Photos are read through the iOS photo picker, so Folia does not need access to your whole photo library. Files are read through the iOS file picker. When you save pictures to your photo library, Folia asks for add-only permission, which lets it add new pictures and does not let it read the rest of your library.

Processing. Edge detection, filters, cropping, text recognition (OCR), PDF creation, merging, splitting, compressing, watermarks, page numbers, signing, password protection and ID card sheets all run on your device, using Apple's Vision, Core Image, VisionKit and PDFKit frameworks. Your documents are not sent to Ecom Swift LLC, to Google, or to any server or AI service, and they are not used to train any model.

Your library. Documents you scan or import are kept in the app's private storage on your device, together with the text recognised on their pages, so you can search them. Deleting a document in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes everything.

Your signature. If you draw a signature, it is saved as a picture in the app's private storage so you can reuse it. You can remove it in Settings. It is only placed on documents when you choose to place it.

Saving and sharing. When you share, save to Files, save to Photos or print, Folia hands the file to iOS. Where it goes next is your choice. We do not receive it.

Passwords. When you protect a PDF, the password you type is used on your device to lock the file and is not stored or sent anywhere. We cannot recover a forgotten password.

3. Information we receive

From the app: none. We do not receive your documents, your identity or your usage of the app.

From you, if you write to us: if you email support@ecomswiftllc.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the request. We keep support emails for as long as needed to help you and to meet our legal duties, and then delete them. Please do not send documents or other sensitive material unless we ask for it.

From the website: the Folia website pages (ecomswiftllc.github.io/folia/) are static pages. They do not use cookies of their own, analytics scripts, advertising scripts or third-party fonts. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operation.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Folia is free and supported by ads shown through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The app shows a banner ad on the Documents screen and, before you save, share or print a finished document, offers one rewarded video ad that you choose to watch. There are no other ads.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and use, according to Google:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, and other device and app identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • information about ads you see and interact with
  • device, operating system and app information and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance

We do not receive this data and we do not combine it with anything we hold. Google's use of the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and on the page "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Some US state privacy laws treat advertising of this kind as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". Folia does not sell your documents. You can limit ad personalisation as described in the next section.

5. Your choices about ads and tracking

  • Consent form (EEA, UK and other regions where required). When you first open the app, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform may appear. You can accept, decline or manage your choices. You can reopen it later in Settings, in the Ad privacy choices row, where one is required.
  • Tracking prompt. After the consent step, iOS may ask whether Folia may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared, and ads you see will be less personalised. The app works the same either way. Folia does not track you itself: the advertising identifier is used only by Google's SDK, and only if you allow it.
  • iOS settings. You can change the tracking choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier in the same area.
  • Ads cannot be switched off inside the app, because ads are how Folia is paid for. If you decline a consent form that applies in your region, ads may not load for you. In that case no banner appears, and when you save, share or print a document the export goes ahead without an ad.
  • Report an ad. If you see an ad that is offensive, misleading or inappropriate, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com. Tell us roughly when and where in the app you saw it and what it showed. A screenshot helps. We review reports and block ad categories or advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.

6. Rewarded video and exports

A rewarded video is shown only if you choose to watch it ("Watch a short ad to export"). One watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes. If you choose Not now, nothing is exported and you can try again at any time. If no ad is available (for example when you are offline, when no ad can be shown, or when your consent choice does not allow ads), the export goes ahead without one. The app remembers that an ad was watched only on your device, for those 2 minutes. This record contains no personal information and is not sent anywhere.

7. Deleting your data

  • Folia has no account. Delete a document inside the app to remove it, or delete the app to remove everything the app stores.
  • Pictures you saved to your photo library, and files you exported, shared or printed, are outside the app. You manage and delete them where you saved or sent them.
  • Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may contain a copy of the app's data according to your Apple settings. That copy is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.
  • We hold no personal data about you from your use of the app, so there is nothing on our side to delete. For advertising data held by Google, use Google's controls (https://myadcenter.google.com) or contact Google.

8. Children

Folia is a general document tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is not designed for the Kids category and does not use child-directed ad treatment. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California and other US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the use of personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information from your use of the app, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you can write to us at support@ecomswiftllc.com about any request and we will respond within the time the law requires. For data held by Google, exercise your rights with Google. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Your documents are protected by the security of your device and by iOS app storage protections. We recommend a device passcode. Scans often contain sensitive details such as names, addresses and account numbers. No method of storage is completely secure, and you are responsible for the files you export and share. Use the password tool to protect a PDF before you send it.

11. International transfers

Google and its partners may process advertising data in the United States and other countries. Where required, they rely on legal transfer mechanisms described in Google's policies.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and where a change is significant we will say so in the app's release notes.
