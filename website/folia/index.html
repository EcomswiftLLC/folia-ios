Everything you do with a PDF

    Point the camera at a page. Edges are found, the page is straightened and the light is evened out.

  • Five clean looks

    Original, Auto, Magic colour, Grayscale and Black and white, switchable any time.

  • Searchable PDFs

Questions

Is Folia really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no paid version. Ads from Google AdMob pay for the app: a banner on the Documents screen, and one rewarded video that you choose to watch before you save, share or print a finished document.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Scanning, text recognition, PDF tools and signing all run on your device. We never receive your documents.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no sign-up.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

Before you export, the app offers one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes. If you choose Not now, nothing is exported and you can try again any time. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline, the export goes ahead anyway.

Can it read the text on a scan?

Yes. Folia reads text on the device and can make the PDF searchable, so you can search and copy words inside it. You can also copy the text of a scan as plain text.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Scanning with the camera needs a device with a camera.

When can I download it?

Folia is not released yet. This page will link to the App Store listing once the app is live.

Who makes Folia?

Ecom Swift LLC, a company registered in Wyoming, United States. You can reach us on the support page.
