Common questions

How do I scan a document?
  1. Open Folia and tap Scan.
  2. Hold the camera over the page. Folia finds the edges and takes the picture for you. Scan more pages if you need to.
  3. Tap Save. Your pages appear in Documents, where you can crop, rotate, change the look and export.
How do I make a PDF from photos I already have?

In Documents, tap the plus button and choose Choose from Photos. Folia looks for the page in each photo and straightens it. You can also choose Import from Files to add PDFs or picture files.

How do I sign a PDF?
  1. Open Tools and choose Sign and fill, or open a document and use the menu, then Sign and fill.
  2. Choose a PDF or one of your scans.
  3. Tap Signature and draw yours with your finger. It is saved on your device for next time.
  4. Drag the signature into place, resize it with the slider, then tap Save signed PDF.
How do I copy the text from a scan?

Open the document, tap the more button and choose Extract text. Folia reads the pages on your device. Copy the text or share it as a text file. When you export a PDF, leave Searchable text on to be able to search inside it.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

Folia is free, and ads pay for it. Before you save, share or print, the app offers one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes. If you choose Not now, nothing is exported and you can try again any time. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline or your ad privacy choice does not allow ads, the export goes ahead without one. Scanning and editing never need an ad.

I forgot the password of a PDF I locked

Folia cannot recover it. The password is not stored anywhere, which is what keeps the file safe. If you still have an unlocked copy, protect it again with a new password.

My scan looks too dark or too light

Open the page and try another look: Auto suits most pages, Black and white suits text, Magic colour suits colour documents. Rotate and crop with the buttons under the page.

I deleted the app and my documents are gone

Folia keeps documents only on your device, so they are removed with the app and we cannot recover them. Export important documents to Files or share them before you delete the app.

I saw an ad that seems wrong

Write to us at the address above. Tell us roughly when and where in the app you saw it and what it showed. A screenshot helps. We block ad categories and advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.

I want to change my ad or tracking choice

Open Settings in the app and look for the Ad privacy choices row, which appears where the law requires it. You can also change tracking in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking.
